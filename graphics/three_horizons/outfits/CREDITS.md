# Imported classic outfit assets

Gold, Kris and Silver graphics are copied from Pokemon Heart & Soul by Lil Dill
and contributors, source revision 751823abaf677020bcd72c45fe3e7cb2b8a576e4:
https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokemonHnS/tree/751823abaf677020bcd72c45fe3e7cb2b8a576e4

Its README invites reuse of its open source and credits sprite artists
Cesare_CBass, AveonTrainer, PurpleZaffre and BatimaTheBat. Per-file authorship
is not specified; this preserves the project's collective attribution.
Underlying Pokemon characters/assets belong to their respective rights holders.

Gold uses people/brendan pose PNGs and trainers/{front_pics,back_pics}/brendan.png.
Kris uses people/may pose PNGs and trainers/{front_pics,back_pics}/may.png.
Silver uses people/special/silver.png and trainers/{front_pics,back_pics}/wally.png.
Files are copied unchanged. Silver's fast movement reuses his walking frames;
future bike, Surf, fishing and field actions currently use Gold's poses. These
are not available in the present opening. Blue's rival graphics remain upstream
FRLG assets. A playable Blue outfit still needs a matching complete sprite set.
Gold/Kris watering images are the source project's fallback poses; inspect before
enabling berry gardening. Original four outfit assets are retained unchanged.
