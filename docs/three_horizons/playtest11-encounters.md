# Playtest 11: wild encounter checklist

Source: release commit `881bf5c492c91698b198bdb9c2590e7e678d3beb`.

This describes the current Playtest 11 tables, not the proposed Playtest 12 additions. Percentages are shares of encounters in that area, not the chance per step. Repels, lead abilities and other encounter modifiers can change what you actually find. The level column lists all configured levels for that species.

Clefairy is 5% on Mt. Moon B2F. Makuhita is 1% on each of its three floors. A 1% encounter can take many attempts; not finding one in 100 encounters does not prove a bug.

Water and fishing tables are included for completeness. A configured table does not mean the required rod, field move or water access is obtainable in Playtest 11.

## Things to record

- Area and floor, clock setting, species, level, ability, and whether a Repel was active.
- First-catch Pokédex opening and closing, both with normal input and repeated A presses.
- Nickname accepted/declined, maximum length, and whether the party was full.
- Hidden Ability is a 10% roll when the species has one; record several catches rather than judging one.

## EV items and nicknames

Power items add 8 EVs in their named stat on top of the defeated species’ normal yield (before relevant multipliers and the 252-per-stat / 510-total caps). Power Lens plus Rattata therefore gives 8 Special Attack and 1 Speed. Macho Brace doubles the normal yield. Duplicate nicknames are allowed.
